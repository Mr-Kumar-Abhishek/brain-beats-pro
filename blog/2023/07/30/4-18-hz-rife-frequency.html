2. Somatic Target: Celiac Disease & Gut-Brain Axis Modulation

A. Understanding the Celiac Inflammatory Pathway

Celiac disease is a chronic autoimmune enteropathy triggered by the ingestion of dietary gluten (specifically gliadin peptides) in genetically predisposed individuals (expressing HLA-DQ2 or HLA-DQ8). When gluten peptides cross the intestinal lamina propria, tissue transglutaminase 2 (tTG-2) deamidates them, provoking:

  1. Intense Mucosal Inflammation: CD4+ helper T-cells and cytotoxic intraepithelial CD8+ lymphocytes attack the intestinal lining.
  2. Villous Atrophy & Crypt Hyperplasia: Flattening of the microvilli leads to severe malabsorption of micronutrients (iron, vitamin B12, vitamin D, calcium).
  3. Loss of Epithelial Barrier Integrity (“Leaky Gut”): Tight junctions disassemble, allowing macromolecules to enter systemic circulation and provoke chronic systemic immune distress.
